# Code of Conduct

This project is used by students and developers all over the world. Be the kind of person you'd want answering your first question.

- Be respectful and assume good faith, including toward people who disagree with a verdict.
- Criticize claims and code, never people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, including by nationality, religion, gender, or experience level.
- Keep vendor debates factual. Fan wars don't belong here.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and block contributors who break these rules. Report problems by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer listed in the repo profile.
