Both Pro and Max plans have a five-hour session limit and a weekly limit. Max plans also have a separate weekly limit for Fable. These limits are shared across Claude and Claude Code, meaning all activity in both tools counts against the same limits. To help you monitor your usage, you’ll see warning messages as you approach any limit.
If you reach your five-hour session limit while Claude Code is partway through a response, Claude Code may keep working briefly so it can finish the step in progress instead of stopping mid-step. While this happens, you’ll see “Usage limit reached · wrapping up” in Claude Code. This wrap-up allowance is small and capped, and the usage counts toward your weekly limit. It applies only to a response that’s already in progress, not to a new message you send after reaching your limit. On the Pro plan, it applies up to once per weekly usage period. On the Max plan, it can apply each time you reach a five-hour session limit, within your weekly limit. It’s rolling out gradually, so it may not be available on your account yet. Learn more about the Claude Code wrap-up allowance .
Enable usage credits to keep using Claude with your Pro plan after you reach your five-hour session limit or your weekly limit.
Enable usage credits to keep using Claude with your Max plan after you reach your five-hour session limit, your general weekly limit, or your separate weekly limit for Fable.
Monthly API credits included with Max plans can’t be used in Claude Code or the Claude apps, including for extra usage. Learn more about monthly API credits for Max and Team plans .
